# KaushalSetu — Scoring, Scalability & Opportunity Analytics

> **Mathematical specifications for deterministic cluster evaluation, demand-supply deficit calculation, learner pathway ranking, and sensitivity perturbation.**

---

## 1. Cluster Scalability Engine (`app/analytics/scalability.py`)

All metrics in KaushalSetu are computed using pure deterministic functions without non-deterministic LLM variance.

### 1.1 Momentum ($M$)
Measures recent investment flow, newly registered MSMEs, and public sector announcements, weighted by an exponential time-decay constant:

$$M = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot \exp\left(-\lambda \cdot \Delta t_i\right)$$

Where:
- $\Delta t_i$ is the age of signal $i$ in days.
- $\lambda = \frac{\ln(2)}{180} \approx 0.00385$ (180-day half-life).
- $w_i$ represents normalized capex scale $\min(1.0, \text{capex\_inr\_cr} / 2500)$.
- Resulting momentum is normalized: $M \in [0.0, 1.0]$.

### 1.2 Cluster Depth ($D$)
Quantifies the density of local micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) active in the corresponding 2-digit and 4-digit NIC codes:

$$D = \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{MSME}_{\text{district, cluster}}}{\text{MSME}_{\text{benchmark}}}\right)$$

Where $\text{MSME}_{\text{benchmark}} = 2,500$ registered units.

### 1.3 Infrastructure Readiness ($I$)
Measures dedicated industrial park acreage (SIPCOT, SIDCO, Private SEZs) and unallocated vacant plots:

$$I = 0.6 \cdot \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{Total Acres}}{2000}\right) + 0.4 \cdot \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{Plots Available}}{20}\right)$$

### 1.4 Policy Tailwinds ($P$)
Reflects targeted state industrial incentives, capital subsidies, and central Production Linked Incentive (PLI) designations:

$$P \in [0.0, 1.0] \quad (\text{default } 0.70 \text{ for priority state green/EV clusters})$$

### 1.5 Composite Scalability Index ($S_{\text{scalability}}$)
The overall cluster viability index:

$$S_{\text{scalability}} = 0.35 \cdot M + 0.25 \cdot D + 0.20 \cdot I + 0.20 \cdot P$$

## 2. Labour Demand, Supply & Deficit Gap

### 2.1 Direct & Induced Demand ($L_{\text{demand}}$)
Forecasts direct job openings generated by committed capex plus confirmed factory announcements:

$$L_{\text{demand}} = \sum \text{Direct Jobs Announced} + \left(\text{Capex (₹ Cr)} \cdot 0.8\right)$$

### 2.2 Local Vocational Supply ($S_{\text{supply}}$)
Annual graduate turnout from relevant ITI trades, polytechnic diplomas, and PMKVY centres:

$$S_{\text{supply}} = \sum_{\text{institutions}} \text{Annual Outturn}_{\text{relevant trades}}$$

### 2.3 Supply Deficit Gap
The mismatch between industry intake requirements and local talent production:

- **Absolute Gap:**
  $$G_{\text{abs}} = \max\left(0, L_{\text{demand}} - S_{\text{supply}}\right)$$

- **Gap Ratio:**
  $$G_{\text{ratio}} = \frac{G_{\text{abs}}}{\max(1, L_{\text{demand}})}$$

## 3. Stage Classification Rules

Clusters are automatically assigned one of four stages based on deterministic rule boundaries:

| Stage | Trigger Criteria | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **`emerging`** | $M \ge 0.60$ AND $I \ge 0.75$ | High capex momentum entering well-provisioned industrial corridors (e.g. EV in Krishnagiri). |
| **`growing`** | $M \ge 0.40$ AND $D \ge 0.50$ | Sustained operational growth with strong local vendor and supplier depth. |
| **`mature`** | $D \ge 0.80$ AND $M < 0.40$ | Established industrial concentration with steady replacement hiring. |
| **`nascent`** | Default fallback | Early announcements with limited physical footprint or supply chain. |

## 4. Opportunity Scoring & Equity Toggle (`app/analytics/opportunity.py`)

### 4.1 Local Priority Mode (`local_priority`)
Prioritizes Tier-2/3 district retention, local job stability, and addressing acute district training deficits:

$$O_{\text{local}} = 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{scalability}} + 0.30 \cdot G_{\text{ratio}} + 0.30 \cdot E_{\text{local}}$$

Where $E_{\text{local}}$ is the district retention equity bonus ($1.0$ for focus district, $0.4$ for state capital comparator).

### 4.2 Market Mode (`market`)
Ranks opportunities primarily by state-wide aggregate hiring volume and absolute salary scale, reflecting unconstrained urban migration patterns:

$$O_{\text{market}} = 0.50 \cdot V_{\text{state\_volume}} + 0.30 \cdot S_{\text{scalability}} + 0.20 \cdot G_{\text{ratio}}$$

Where $V_{\text{state\_volume}}$ is the state-wide vacancy percentile of the job role (e.g., high in metropolitan tech/finance centres).

## 5. Learner Pathway Ranking (`app/services/recommendation.py`)

Given an anonymous profile $\mathcal{P} = (\text{education}, \text{skills}, \text{district}, \text{max\_travel\_km})$:

### 5.1 Skill Fit ($\text{Fit}_{\text{skill}}$)
Semantic and keyword overlap between learner credentials and role requirements:

$$\text{Fit}_{\text{skill}} = \frac{|\text{Skills}_{\text{learner}} \cap \text{Skills}_{\text{role}}|}{|\text{Skills}_{\text{role}}|}$$

### 5.2 Distance & Access Decay ($\text{Access}$)
Exponential friction applied to commute distance $d$ in kilometers:

$$\text{Access}(d) = \exp\left(-\frac{d}{40\text{ km}}\right)$$

If $d > \text{max\_travel\_km}$, an additional $0.5$ penalty factor is applied.

### 5.3 Composite Route Score ($R$)
$$R = 0.35 \cdot \text{Fit}_{\text{skill}} + 0.25 \cdot \text{Access} + 0.20 \cdot O_{\text{mode}} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Eligibility}$$

---

## 6. Sensitivity Analysis Grid
The sensitivity engine evaluates ranking robustness by perturbing weights across a grid $\Delta w \in \{-0.10, 0.0, +0.10\}$ and re-normalizing such that $\sum w_i = 1.0$.

If a cluster maintains top-1 ranking under $\ge 80\%$ of weight permutations, it is designated **Robust Top Priority**.
